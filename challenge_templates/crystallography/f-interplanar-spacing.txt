[topic]
name: Interplanar spacing

[question]
type: d-cubic
text: A cubic crystal has the lattice parameter a = {a}. What is the spacing d of the {plane} planes? Give 3 significant figures.
explain: For cubic crystals $d = \dfrac{a}{\sqrt{h^2+k^2+l^2}}$: {calc}.

[question]
type: d-tetragonal
text: A tetragonal crystal has a = {a} and c = {c}. What is the spacing d of the {plane} planes? Give 3 significant figures.
explain: For tetragonal crystals $\dfrac{1}{d^2} = \dfrac{h^2+k^2}{a^2} + \dfrac{l^2}{c^2}$: {calc}.

[question]
type: d-hexagonal
text: A hexagonal crystal has a = {a} and c = {c}. What is the spacing d of the {plane} planes? Give 3 significant figures.
explain: For hexagonal crystals $\dfrac{1}{d^2} = \dfrac{4}{3}\cdot\dfrac{h^2+hk+k^2}{a^2} + \dfrac{l^2}{c^2}$ (with four indices (hkil), leave out i): {calc}.
