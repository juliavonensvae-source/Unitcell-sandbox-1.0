[question]
type: identify-2d
level: medium, hard
text: Which 2D Bravais lattice do these lattice points form?
explain: {why}

[words]
picture: A two-dimensional lattice of points
why-mp: Oblique: the shortest lattice vectors have different lengths, the angle between them is neither 90° nor 120°, and no rectangle with a centre point fits the pattern. There are no mirror lines.
why-op: Rectangular: the points form rectangles (90° angles, two different side lengths) with no point in the middle.
why-oc: Centred rectangular: the points form rectangles with an extra point in the middle of each. The primitive cell is a rhombus: equal sides, but an angle that is not 60°, 90° or 120°.
why-tp: Square: equal sides at 90°.
why-hp: Hexagonal: equal sides at 120°. Every point has six nearest neighbours at the same distance.

answer: It is the same lattice as a primitive square lattice with a smaller cell, turned by 45°
wrong: The centre point would destroy the 4-fold symmetry | It is the same lattice as the hexagonal lattice | It is the same lattice as the centred rectangular lattice
