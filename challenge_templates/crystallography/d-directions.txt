# QUESTION TYPES IN THIS TOPIC (levels · how it is answered)
#   dir-between-points-cubic     easy, medium, hard · multiple choice or typed [uvw]
#   dir-between-points-hex       easy, medium, hard · multiple choice or typed [UVW]
#       {A} the tail · {B} the head · {diff} B − A · {clear} the step to whole numbers
#       (words clear / no-clear). On hexagonal axes the coordinates are in units of a1, a2, c.
#   dir-between-points-hex4      hard · multiple choice or typed [uvtw]
#       as above, and {three} the direction in three indices
#   dir-family-member-cubic      easy, medium, hard · multiple choice
#   dir-family-not-member-cubic  easy, medium, hard · multiple choice
#       {family} the family, e.g. ⟨110⟩
#   dir-family-count-cubic       easy, medium, hard · multiple choice or typed (a number)
#       {family} · {members} every member · {count} how many
#       A direction and its negative count as TWO members: ⟨100⟩ has 6, ⟨111⟩ has 8.
#   dir-family-member-hex        medium, hard · multiple choice (four indices)
#   dir-family-count-hex         medium, hard · multiple choice or typed (a number)
#       {family} · {members} · {count} as above, in four-index notation

[question]

text: In a hexagonal cell, with coordinates in units of a1, a2 and c, what is the direction [UVW] (three indices) from point A = {A} to point B = {B}?

[question]
type: dir-between-points-hex4
text: In a hexagonal cell, with coordinates in units of a1, a2 and c, what is the direction from point A = {A} to point B = {B}? Give it in four-index notation [uvtw].
explain: B − A = {diff}. {clear} In three indices that is {three}. Convert with u = (2U − V)/3, v = (2V − U)/3, t = −(u + v), w = W, then clear the fractions and reduce: {answer}.

[question]

explain: The directions of {family} are the orderings and sign changes of the same three numbers. {answer} uses the same numbers; the others don't.

[question]

[question]
type: dir-family-count-cubic
text: How many different directions belong to the family {family} in a cubic crystal? Count a direction and its negative, e.g. [1 1 0] and [−1 −1 0], separately: they point opposite ways.
explain: Every ordering and sign change of the indices: {members}. That makes {count}.

[question]
type: dir-family-member-hex

explain: In four-index notation the members of {family} come from reordering the first three indices, changing the sign of all three together, and changing the sign of the last index. {answer} is one of them; the others are not.

[question]
type: dir-family-count-hex
text: How many different directions belong to the family {family} in a hexagonal crystal? Count a direction and its negative separately.
explain: Reorder the first three indices, change their signs together, and change the sign of the last: {members}. That makes {count}.
