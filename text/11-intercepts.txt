# Plane intercepts card
interceptsTitle: Intercepts
interceptsMenu: Plane intercepts a/h, b/k, c/l
interceptsPicker: Intercepts of the plane
interceptsNote: Plane n cuts the axes at n/h, n/k and n/l (in units of a, b and c). An index of 0 means the plane never meets that axis: it is parallel to it.
interceptsNoteFour: Plane n cuts each axis at n/h, n/k, n/i and n/l (in units of a<sub>1</sub>, a<sub>2</sub>, a<sub>3</sub> and c). An index of 0 means the plane never meets that axis: it is parallel to it.

## Steps table
interceptsStep1: 1 · Intercepts
interceptsStep2: 2 · Reciprocals
interceptsStep3: 3 · Clear fractions <small>({factor})</small>
interceptsWhole: already whole numbers
interceptsStep4: 4 · Miller indices: <b>{indices}</b>
interceptsSameSet: Plane n = {n} of {indices} is also a plane of the {cleared} set: {indices} has the same planes plus extra ones in between. Plane n = 1 gives back {indices}.

## Plane number and origin
interceptsLevel: Plane number n
interceptsNearest: (nearest)
interceptsWhereCell: O = ({origin}), a corner of cell ({cell})
interceptsWherePrism: O = ({origin}) in prism ({cell})
interceptsPrismOrigin: The origin O is the centre of the bottom face of the highlighted prism, {where}. A negative intercept simply lies on the other side of O.
interceptsAuto: Choose the best corner automatically
interceptsAutoHint: {where}: the corner of the highlighted cell that brings plane n = 1 inside it.
interceptsManualHint: {where}: the origin chosen in the Add cells drawer (right edge).
interceptsOriginNote: Planes with a negative index cut that axis behind the origin. Moving O to another corner of the cell brings plane n = 1 inside the cell. A larger n shows how fractions appear and are cleared.
interceptsOutside: With this origin the plane meets some axes outside the cell. Turn on the automatic corner, or pick another corner in the Add cells drawer.
interceptsPrismHint: Choose <b>Hexagonal prism</b> in the Add cells drawer (right edge) to see the a<sub>3</sub> intercept drawn as well.
