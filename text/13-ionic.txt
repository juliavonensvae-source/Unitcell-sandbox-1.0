# Ionic structures: names and descriptions
ionicNameNaCl: rock salt
ionicHolesNaCl: Na⁺ fills every octahedral hole of the Cl⁻ array
ionicOthersNaCl: MgO, LiF, KCl, AgCl
ionicNameCsCl: caesium chloride
ionicHolesCsCl: Cs⁺ sits in the cubic hole at the centre of each Cl⁻ cube
ionicOthersCsCl: CsBr, CsI, TlCl
ionicNameZnS: zinc blende
ionicHolesZnS: Zn²⁺ fills half of the tetrahedral holes of the S²⁻ array
ionicOthersZnS: GaAs, CuCl, β-SiC
ionicNameCaF2: fluorite
ionicHolesCaF2: F⁻ fills every tetrahedral hole of the Ca²⁺ array
ionicOthersCaF2: UO₂, ZrO₂ (cubic), BaF₂
