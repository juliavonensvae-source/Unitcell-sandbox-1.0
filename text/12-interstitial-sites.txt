# Kinds of site
siteOctName: Octahedral
siteOctAdj: octahedral
siteTetName: Tetrahedral
siteTetAdj: tetrahedral
siteCubName: Cubic
siteCubAdj: cubic

## Where the sites are
siteWhereCP: the body centre
siteWhereCIOct: the face centres and edge midpoints
siteWhereCITet: (½, ¼, 0)-type positions on the faces
siteWhereCFOct: the body centre and edge midpoints
siteWhereCFTet: (¼, ¼, ¼)-type positions inside the cell

## Places in a cell
placeBodyCentre: body centre
placeInside: inside the cell
placeFaceCentre: face centre
placeOnFace: on a face
placeEdgeMidpoint: edge midpoint
placeOnEdge: on an edge
placeCorner: corner

## Shapes the neighbours form (also used by ionic structures)
polyOctahedron: octahedron
polyTetrahedron: tetrahedron
polyCube: cube
polyTriangle: triangle
polyLine: line
