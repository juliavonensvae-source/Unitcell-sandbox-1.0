## + Add category
catSlip: Close-packed planes

## + Add entries and command help (plain text: no HTML)
slipMenuFcc: FCC (cubic F): {111} planes
slipMenuFccDesc: Truly close-packed: each atom touches 6 in its plane. 12 slip systems, {111}⟨110⟩. Switches to cubic F.
slipMenuBcc: BCC (cubic I): {110} planes
slipMenuBccDesc: The densest planes, but not close-packed: each atom touches 4. Slip along ⟨111⟩. Switches to cubic I.
slipMenuSc: SC (cubic P): {100} planes
slipMenuScDesc: A square net, each atom touching 4. Slip along ⟨100⟩, as for Edge and Screw. Switches to cubic P.
cmdHelpSlip: Slip on the densest planes, with a dislocation that glides across: FCC, BCC or SC (nothing: the lattice on screen)

## Card
slipTitle: Slip on a close-packed plane
slipSC: SC
slipBCC: BCC
slipFCC: FCC
slipIntro: The densest planes of {structure} and the directions it slips along. The layer just below one slip plane is orange, the layer just above it blue.
slipCubicOnly: Slip planes are drawn in cubic P, I and F, and {lattice} is on screen. Switch to one of them:
slipCubicShort: Cubic P, I and F only
slipBefore: No dislocation in the crystal yet, so it is perfect. Press <b>Glide across</b> to send one over the plane, or drag the slider.
slipIn: Where the line has passed, the layer above has slipped by <b>b</b> over the layer below; ahead of it, not yet. Only the atoms near the line are out of place.
slipInPartials: The leading partial moves the atoms above by <b>b₁</b> into the empty hollows next door, the trailing one on by <b>b₂</b>. Between the two lines (green) the stacking is wrong: a stacking fault.
slipAfter: The dislocation has crossed: everything above the plane has slipped by b = {b} Å, the crystal is perfect again, and a step of that height shows on each side.
slipSystem: Slip system
slipSystemsCount: {count} systems ({planes} planes × {dirs} directions)
slipPlaneSpacing: Slip plane
slipInPlane: In the plane
slipInPlaneValue: each atom touches <b>{n}</b> · {packing} covered
slipBurgers: Burgers vector
slipLine: Line along
slipLineEdge: edge: at right angles to b
slipLineScrew: screw: along b
slipPartialsRow: Shockley partials
slipPlaneLabel: Slip plane (one of {count})
slipDirLabel: Slip direction b (one of {count} in the plane)
slipCharacter: Dislocation
slipEdge: Edge
slipScrew: Screw
slipPartials: Split into two Shockley partials
slipPartialsHint: The atoms above take the easy way, through the empty hollow between the atoms below, in two smaller steps b₁ and b₂.
slipWidth: Distance between the partials (× |b|)
slipWidthHint: Set by hand so that both fit in the block; in copper they are about 6 to 12 |b| apart.
slipWhere: Where the dislocation is
slipGlide: Glide across
slipStart: Back to the start
slipGlideHintEdge: The shear stress τ pushes the line along b: it enters on one side, crosses the plane and leaves a slip step of b.
slipGlideHintScrew: The shear stress τ pushes the line sideways, at right angles to b: once it has crossed, the two halves have slipped by b along the line.
slipLook: Look
slipLookAngle: At an angle
slipLookPlane: Straight at the plane
slipLookLine: Along the line
slipRest: The rest of the crystal
slipRestShow: Show
slipRestFaint: Faint
slipRestHide: Hide
slipSize: Size of the layer atoms (100 % = touching)
slipSizeHint: At 100 % the atoms touch as in a real crystal; smaller, the slip plane, the line and the stacking fault show between them.
slipShowPlane: Show the slip plane
slipShowPlaneHint: Darker where the dislocation has already passed, so the atoms there have slipped.
slipShowDirs: Show the close-packed directions
slipShowDirsHint: Beside the block, in the plane: every direction along which the atoms touch. b is one of them.
slipRoseLabel: close-packed directions in {plane}
slipShowPath: Show the path of one atom
slipShowPathHint: An atom of the layer above moves by b when the dislocation passes.
slipShowPathHintFcc: An atom of the layer above moves by b; with partials it goes by b₁ and then b₂ (dashed: the straight way, over an atom below).
slipShowShear: Show the shear stress τ
slipShowShearHint: The stress on the slip plane, along b, that makes the line glide; Glide turns it on.
slipAddNote: {structure}: the {plane} slip plane and the layers either side of it. Press Glide across on its card to send a dislocation over it.
slipAddNoteColour: The atoms are now one colour, so the two layers stand out; change it on the Atoms card.
slipSummary: {plane} · <i>b</i> = {b} · {kind}
