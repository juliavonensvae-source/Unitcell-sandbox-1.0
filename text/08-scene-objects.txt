pointsSummarySpheres: {count} touching spheres · r = {radius} Å
pointsSummaryCircles: {count} touching circles · r = {radius} Å
pointsShowAs: Show as
pointsPoints: Points
pointsSpheres: Touching spheres
pointsRadiusNote: Radius r = d<sub>nn</sub>/2 = {radius} Å, so each sphere just touches its nearest neighbours.

pointsSizeTouching: Size (100% = touching)
pointsCut: Cut spheres at cell boundary
pointsCutHint: Cut at the highlighted cell: corners show as ⅛, edges ¼, faces ½. In slanted cells the pieces differ in size but still add up to whole spheres.

pointsSolid: Solid
pointsHollow: Hollow

pointsHollowHint: Hollow: you look into the empty shell of each cut sphere.
pointsCutLater: Takes effect when spheres are cut.
pointsBonds: Bonds to nearest neighbours
