# Short descriptions
# Shown under an entry in the + Add menu, and at the top of the matching card.
descLatticePoints: Every lattice point has identical surroundings. Show them as small dots, or as spheres just large enough to touch their nearest neighbours.
descSupercell: Repeat the unit cell along a, b and c to see how cells stack together into the lattice.
descDirection: An arrow from the origin to the point u·a + v·b + w·c. Negative indices are written with a bar, e.g. [1̅10].
descPlane: A lattice plane that cuts the axes at a/h, b/k and c/l. An index of 0 means the plane is parallel to that axis.
descSpacing: The perpendicular distance between two neighbouring planes of a set, drawn as a dimension line. In centred lattices extra lattice points can lie between the planes from the formula, so the planes that really hold points are closer together.
descIntercepts: Where a plane cuts the a, b and c axes, measured from an origin at a cell corner, and how intercepts → reciprocals → clear fractions gives (hkl). An index of 0 means the plane is parallel to that axis.
descComponents: Walk u steps along a, then v along b, then w along c to reach the tip of [uvw]. Each corner of the path is labelled with its coordinates.
descInterstitials: The empty spaces between lattice spheres: octahedral and tetrahedral holes (cubic sites in cubic P) where smaller atoms can sit. Shows how many there are per cell and per lattice point.
descAtomFit: Put an atom of radius r in a hole: green when it fits (r/R up to the critical ratio), red with the overlap shown when it is too big.
descVolume: The space enclosed by the unit cell. The general formula works for every crystal system; it simplifies a lot when the angles are 90°.
descPoints: A point on a corner is shared by 8 cells, on an edge by 4, on a face by 2; a point inside belongs to this cell alone.
descPacking: The fraction of the cell filled by touching spheres, with radius half the nearest-neighbour distance.
descNN: The distance to the closest lattice points (d_nn) and how many there are (the coordination number, CN).
descDSpacing: The perpendicular distance between neighbouring planes of the (hkl) set. For cubic cells d = a / √(h² + k² + l²).
descBragg: X-rays reflect strongly from a set of planes when the path difference between neighbouring planes is a whole number of wavelengths.
descMetric: G collects all dot products of the cell vectors, Gij = ai · aj. Lengths, angles and d-spacings in any system follow from G and its inverse G*.
descAdvanced: Unlocks lattice parameter editing, Miller–Bravais notation, the X-ray wavelength, projection settings and extra calculations.
